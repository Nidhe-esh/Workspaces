## 🚀 HOW TO GET STARTED (3 EASY STEPS)

### Step 1: Run the App
```
Navigate to: e:\Projects\Workspaces\workspaces\dist\
Double-click: Workspaces.exe
```

### Step 2: Scan Your Desktop
Click "Scan Workspace" — it will show all your running apps.

### Step 3: Save & Restore
- Enter a workspace name and click "Save"
- Close some apps
- Select your workspace and click "Restore"
- ✅ Done! All apps relaunch!
